# Sorting Course browser

The 100-level Sorting Course now opens a dedicated browser instead of a single long menu. Levels are grouped into four sets of 25, with one card per level and clear marks for the current level, completion and every-fifth-level Discovery boards.

Completed cards can also show whether the most recent successful attempt used a hint or helper vessel. Those marks are explicitly informational: they do not affect completion, progress or access to any level. Every level remains directly selectable.
