# Five-minute tester check

1. Open Vials Fluid Lab (the teal pouring-vial icon). Select Green arrival from the puzzle menu for the first six-vial, three-color level.
2. Play a few moves in Fluid / Quick, then switch to Classic between moves. Check that the puzzle, move count and Undo agree.
3. Finish the puzzle using Hint when useful, then select Next. Later, return to the earlier puzzle and check its saved state.
4. Try the optional pouring sound in Board options. Compare High and Low energy detail at the same pace. Choose whichever looks best for normal play.
5. Report which presentation and pace you prefer, whether Leaf green is easy to distinguish, and whether any pour feels distracting, slow, jerky or visibly pulled toward its receiver.

For manual measurements, open Board diagnostics → Record session, play normally, then Finish measurement. Share measurements exports a local JSON file. Finish manual recordings before locking the device; move durations are wall-clock durations and include pauses.
