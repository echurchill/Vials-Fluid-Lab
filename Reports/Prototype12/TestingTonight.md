# Tonight’s presentation comparison

Allow about 10–15 minutes per tester. Use the same iPad and leave brightness unchanged during the comparison.

1. Play a few moves in 2D Fluid at Quick pace. Include a single-unit pour and a pour into a nearly full vial. Watch the source leaving, the stream landing, and both liquid levels after settling.
2. After a representative move, open **Board options → Compare last pour**. Keep **Match duration** on and replay Classic, 2D Fluid and 3D Fluid. Each starts from the same captured board; the preview does not change your game. Alternate the order between testers.
3. Turn **Match duration** off and repeat to judge the actual Quick pace. Keep appearance and pacing preferences separate.
4. Return to the board and try Pause/Resume, Undo, and one invalid destination. Confirm it is obvious what happened and what can be tapped next.

Record a few short answers:

- Overall preference: Classic / 2D Fluid / 3D Fluid / undecided.
- Which is easiest to read? Which is most enjoyable to watch?
- At normal Quick pace: too slow / about right / too fast, for each mode.
- Did any single-unit pour clump, liquid level jump, or particle escape stand out? Note the puzzle, source → destination and mode.
- Any visible freeze, missed tap, warmth, or noticeable battery drop? Approximate time and whether the iPad was plugged in help us investigate. A short battery-gauge observation alone is not an energy measurement.

Keep sound as it is for tonight. Prefer concrete examples over an overall score; one repeatable awkward pour is especially useful.
